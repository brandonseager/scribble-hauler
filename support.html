Scribble Hauler Support

Pile it high. Drive it careful.

Found a bug, stuck on a route, or have an idea? Email bseager21@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and the route number.

How to play

Hold the right pedal to drive and the left to brake or reverse. In the air, the pedals tip the nose up or down. Deliver at least the number of pieces shown to clear a route; deliver everything for three stars.

Common questions

I can't get up a hill. Heavy loads sap your climbing. Try grippier tires or stronger suspension in the Garage, or take a run at it.

There's no sound. Check the ringer switch and volume, and make sure Sound is on (title screen or pause menu).

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Ads. Ads appear only on menu screens, never while you drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.
