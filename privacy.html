Privacy Policy

Scribble Hauler · Effective October 28, 2026

Scribble Hauler is a game made by Brandon Seager ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress (unlocked routes, stars, coins and garage upgrades) and your sound setting are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The free version of Scribble Hauler shows banner ads on its menu screens, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance

When the app first opens, you may be asked for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), and iOS will ask whether the app may track you. If you choose not to allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and, where offered, through the "Privacy choices" button on the title screen.

Google's use of this information is governed by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Scribble Hauler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
